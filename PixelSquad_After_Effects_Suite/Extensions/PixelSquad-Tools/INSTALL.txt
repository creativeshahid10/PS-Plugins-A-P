PS Tools - Installation Guide
=============================================

1. Copy the extension folder into your Adobe CEP extensions directory:

   For Windows:
   C:\Program Files (x86)\Common Files\Adobe\CEP\extensions\
   (OR: C:\Users\<UserName>\AppData\Roaming\Adobe\CEP\extensions\)

   For macOS:
   /Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/
   (OR: ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/)

2. Restart After Effects.
3. Open from menu: Window > Extensions > PS Tools
