PixelSquad Suite Privacy Policy

100% Offline-First Architecture & Data Protection Standard · Updated November 2026

Core Guarantee 100% Local & Offline Processing

PixelSquad Suite (including PixelSquad ULTIMATE, PixelSquad GUI Pro, PixelSquad Captions, and PixelSquad Wheel) is engineered with an uncompromising offline-first philosophy. Your video clips, audio stems, timeline sequences, project bins, and artistic media never leave your local workstation.

  • Local Whisper AI Speech-to-Text: All automatic captioning and audio transcription tasks run exclusively on your local CPU / CUDA GPU using local open-source models. No audio is ever transmitted to any third-party cloud.
  • Zero Telemetry & Tracking: PixelSquad does not log keystrokes, creative actions, timeline statistics, or file metadata. There are no background analytics pings or advertising trackers.
  • Air-Gapped Operation: All creative tools, radial selectors, keyframe curve interpolators, and cut detection algorithms remain fully operational without an internet connection.

Local Device Configuration

To provide a seamless editing experience, PixelSquad stores user preferences (theme colors, custom layout arrangements, favorite effects, and local Python interpreter paths) solely within your local operating system directory:

  • ~/.pixelsquad/ and local Adobe CEP HTML5 storage for user interface presets.
  • No account credentials, personal identities, or financial payment details are ever requested, recorded, or retained by the extension panels.
