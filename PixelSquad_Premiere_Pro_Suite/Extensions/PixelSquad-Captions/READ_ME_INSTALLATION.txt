                 PIXEL SQUAD - PSCAPTIONS MASTER SUITE v1.0
          Viral Animated Captions & Subtitles Panel for Premiere Pro
==============================================================================

PSCaptions Adobe Premiere Pro (CC 2020 through CC 2026+) ke liye ek powerful,
dedicated animated captions aur kinetic typography panel hai.

FEATURES:
- 12 Curated Animated Presets in 6 Categories:
  * Word-by-Word (Reels / Shorts)
  * Hormozi Bounce (Yellow Pop, Green Scale)
  * Neon Glow (Cyber Cyan, Vaporwave Magenta)
  * Minimal Clean (Editorial Sub, Clean Lower)
  * Box Highlight (Rounded Pill, Red Accent)
  * Kinetic Pop (Spring Up, Slide Impact)
- 9:16 Vertical Reels/Shorts & 16:9 Horizontal YouTube formats.
- Dual Mode:
  (A) Single Punchline Mode: Playhead par 1-click animated word/phrase.
  (B) Bulk SRT / Transcript Mode: Poori .SRT file paste karke pure video
      ke timestamps par automatically animated captions place karein.
- 10 Curated viral caption fonts bundled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS (WINDOWS):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Agar aapne ZIP download ki hai, toh pehle ZIP par Right-Click karke
   "Extract All" (Sabhi Extract Karein) karein.
2. Extracted folder me jayein aur "1-CLICK_INSTALL_WINDOWS.bat" par double-click karein.
3. Installation complete hone par Adobe Premiere Pro open karein.
4. Premiere Pro me jayein:
   Window -> Extensions -> PSCaptions
5. PSCaptions panel open ho jayega aur aapke saare presets ready hain!

UNINSTALLATION:
Agar aap panel ko remove karna chahte hain, toh "UNINSTALL_WINDOWS.bat" run karein.
